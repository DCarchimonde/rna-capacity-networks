DATA AND CODE MAP

SCIENTIFIC MODULES
analyses/independent_pair/
  GSE166555 count preparation, same-cell H and cross-cell U, repeatability,
  patient intervals and epithelial-subtype decomposition.
analyses/molecular_resolution/
  Fixed-k targets, exact conditional-flow kernels, graph examples, controlled
  simulations and patient resolution spectra.
analyses/strong_comparators/
  Exact R8 multinomial plug-in and pinned CS-CORE implementation. Native fit
  failures and convergence are recorded; hybrid replacement is a separate
  sensitivity analysis. The original third-party licence is in vendor/LICENSE.
analyses/review_completion/
  Occupancy-stratified integration for the 26-gene graph; sparse, near-tie,
  threshold and background analyses; patient intervals and held-out MMR models.
  inputs/ contains the complete atlas projection.
analyses/motif_extension/
  Bivariate-sufficiency checks, independent-edge integration, shared-capacity
  decomposition, numerical refinement and fixed-bivariate simulations. The
  Wald output is diagnostic; permutation results provide primary detection.
analyses/second_round/
  Paired D_G/R500 permutation analysis, deletion-edge identities and sparse-bound
  enumeration. cache/ contains fixed evaluation counts and component lookups.
  inputs/permutation_groups.npz fixes the original randomization categories.
integration/propagate_refinement.py
  Applies the final fixed refinement to full-graph scores and thresholds.
analysis/review_diagnostics.py
  Depth, occupancy and native CS-CORE comparisons on common converged patients.
scripts/verify_equations_independent.py
  Separate rational and symbolic checks of graph and sampling identities.
scripts/check_results.py
  Count identity, component aggregation and manuscript-result consistency.
