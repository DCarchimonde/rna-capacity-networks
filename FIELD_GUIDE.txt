Guide to principal numeric fields

Use the headers and analysis specifications for each table. SOURCE_DATA_INDEX.txt
maps the result families and lists their field names.

k: molecular sampling resolution.
R or R_k: retained-flow ratio after the worst single-edge deletion.
A0 or A_0: intact mean component flow.
A_<edge>: mean component flow after deleting the named edge.
dominant_edge or bottleneck: edge with the smallest deletion component flow.
R_ind: the independent-edge comparator where that label is present.
D: consult the table's specification; D_G denotes shared-capacity loss in the
decomposition, whereas cohort label D denotes GSE178341 in original analyses.
cells, patients: analysis-specific retained counts, not total atlas size.
MMRd and MMRp: source-study mismatch-repair deficient and proficient labels.
all_pairs, opposite_pairs, unresolved_pairs: pair-count denominators and outcomes.
Probabilities and rates are fractions unless the field or specification says
otherwise; multiply fractions by 100 for percent and differences by 100 for
percentage-point contrasts. Flow scores are not percentages by default.
An interval label retains its stated uncertainty source (simulation, cell
bootstrap, patient bootstrap or integration Monte Carlo).

Count matrices are cached raw integer projections with all-feature RNA totals.
The full original public atlas HDF5 is not included; the archive contains its
26-gene projection, metadata, URL and SHA-256 identity.
